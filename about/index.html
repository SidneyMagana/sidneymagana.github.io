About / The story behind the work

From the Ring of Fire.
To worlds ahead.

El Salvador is a land of volcanoes, part of the Pacific Ring of Fire. That landscape is part of my heritage—and it gives me a fitting way to explain what I do: I’m drawn to beautiful possibilities, and I’m not afraid of a little fire.

My work has taken me into the kinds of complicated situations people call “dumpster fires.” At Google, I helped turn reactive merchant support into clearer documentation and prevention workflows. At Amazon, I helped rethink sorting layouts when demand surged. I learned to investigate what was really happening, bring people together, and make the next move practical.

I studied anthropology to understand how people create meaning, use tools, and shape culture. Today that perspective guides the worlds I imagine, the visual stories I direct, and the questions I ask about AI as a creative tool. The volcano film beside this story is a creative interpretation of that connection—not documentary footage. I love the imaginative part of the work, but I know how to handle the messy middle, too.
