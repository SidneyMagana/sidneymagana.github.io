How I work

Listen closely. Make it clear.
Carry it through.

  1. 01 / Understand

    I listen, gather the details, and find out what people need to accomplish.

  2. 02 / Connect

    I trace the missing information, decisions, and people involved.

  3. 03 / Make

    I turn the findings into a plan, workflow, explanation, or creative direction.

  4. 04 / Refine

    I review what works, address the gaps, and keep the next step clear.

Where AI fits in my process

I use AI to explore creative ideas and support parts of my production and organization process, including social assets, video-editing preparation, notes, calendar tasks, and portfolio development. I select, check, and revise the results. These are hands-on experiments in my own practice; each project explains its own tools and evidence.

My earlier partner-support work also included knowledge-base development and chatbot training with colleagues. That work has its own case study.

A practical tutorial: from a scattered idea to a useful brief
  1. Name the decision. Who is this for, and what should they understand or do?
  2. Gather the facts. Separate what I know from what I need to ask.
  3. Write a short brief. Define the message, output, constraints, and review criteria.
  4. Explore options. Use sketches, drafts, or AI where useful, then explain my selection.
  5. Check and deliver. Review accuracy, readability, permissions, accessibility, and the final format.
