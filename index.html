About Sidney

I make complex things easier to use.

I’m Sidney Magaña, a Bay Area AI Creative Strategist, freelance content creator, and human-centered digital storyteller.

I help e-commerce and marketing teams solve a familiar problem: people can’t act on information they don’t understand. I turn complex products and processes into clear content, practical workflows, and digital experiences people can use.

My background in anthropology and cross-cultural research taught me to listen closely to how people describe a problem. Supporting Google’s retail partners through Artech taught me how to follow that problem through the data, the technology, and the teams responsible for fixing it. Today I bring both to AI-assisted content strategy and creative work.
