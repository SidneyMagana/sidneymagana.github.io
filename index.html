From Forgotten Fabric to New Form

An experiment in AI-assisted fashion ideation, material reuse, and creative iteration.

Creative DirectionGenerative AIFashion IdeationAdobe Express
01 / The concept

A piece with memories, reimagined.

I started with a beloved silk scarf that had seen better days but still held meaning. Instead of letting it go, I asked what new forms its color, pattern, and drape might take.

Using AI-assisted visualization, I explored five distinct silhouettes—from a structured spaghetti-strap crop top to an asymmetrical scarf top. My role was to guide the creative direction, compare possibilities, and preserve the character of the original material.

02 / The transformation

One scarf. Five directions.

Original scarf and AI-assisted garment concept explorations supplied by the creator. These are design visualizations, not verified physically constructed garments.

05 / Reflection

AI expands possibilities. People make choices.

The outcome is a curated collection of five speculative garment directions, not a claim of physical production or verified environmental savings.

This exercise sharpened my approach to AI-assisted design: begin with an intentional constraint, explore multiple directions, then use human judgment to shape what deserves to be shown.
