03 / Work experience

Making maintenance requests easier to track

In affordable housing operations, I helped move work-order tracking into software while keeping paper requests available for the maintenance team.

Read the workflow decision

Paper worked for the people doing the repairs. Software gave the office a way to track the request and follow up. Keeping both made it possible to improve visibility without changing how the maintenance team received its work.
