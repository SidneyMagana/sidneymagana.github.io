Brand strategy / Identity system

Back to El Salvador

A cultural and travel storytelling brand about Salvadoran belonging, everyday life, and the many ways people return.

@backtoelsalvador · Proud · Intimate · Generous · Discerning

A return with room for your story.

01 / Positioning

Audience

Salvadoran diaspora, especially people negotiating a relationship with home; independent travelers interested in locally grounded stories.

Promise

A thoughtful connection to place that leaves room for different experiences of identity.

02 / Logo system

Current open-doorway and horizon logo. Keep it separate from the other brands; avoid generic flags or tropical emblems.

04 / Typography

Source Serif Pro headlines; Acumin Pro body. Use licensed webfonts or readable fallbacks.

05 / Art direction & motion

Imagery and layout

Own photography, specific places, family tables, landscape, architecture, and people who consent to appear. Editorial breathing room and natural skin tones. Luxury means attentive detail, not invented facilities.

Motion and accessibility

Quiet editorial movement only where useful; preserve reduced-motion accessibility.

06 / Voice & messaging

Warm, proud, nuanced. Use return, place, welcome, memory, discover. Avoid untouched paradise, hidden natives, or claims to represent every Salvadoran.

Coming back can mean something different every time. There is room for that here.

07 / Content pillars

01 / Diaspora stories
02 / Local voices
03 / Practical cultural context
04 / Personal travel notes
05 / Proposed hospitality development

08 / Priority assets & CTA

First assets: Editorial cover · Diaspora story carousel · Destination field note · Interest page · Concept newsletter

Primary CTA: Follow the journey or register interest

09 / Brand boundaries

Hospitality, amenities, activities, and partnerships remain proposed until confirmed. Link Venite Voz only when an actual service answers a reader’s need; do not imply a partnership.

Brand identity guidelines describe creative direction; they are not proof of business results, service availability, or independent validation.
