Questions / Answers

Good to know.

What is Back to El Salvador?

A storytelling project exploring Salvadoran diaspora, culture, travel, and the many meanings of returning home.

Can I book a stay, tour, or experience?

Yes. To request a booking, email us or DM on Instagram with your preferred dates, number of guests, and what you would like to book. We will reply with availability and details. Airbnb and other online listings may be added later.

How do I make a booking inquiry?

Email backtoelsalvador@gmail.com or send a direct message to @backtoelsalvador. Please include your preferred dates, number of guests, and any questions. We will confirm availability and booking arrangements directly.

Is this only for Salvadorans living abroad?

No. Diaspora perspectives are central, but thoughtful travelers and anyone interested in locally grounded Salvadoran stories are welcome.

Will you feature community voices?

That is part of the editorial direction. We will seek consent before sharing identifiable people's stories or photographs.

How can I collaborate or share my story?

Send an email with a short introduction and what you would like to contribute. We welcome thoughtful ideas without promising publication.
