05 / Art direction & motion

Imagery and layout

Spatial creative studio suspended above a dark ocean; chrome seed-node sculpture, translucent panels, restrained depth, and volcanic material meeting liquid light. Mostly dark space, one blue focal element, and small lava accents. Keep blue and red light fields separate.

Motion and accessibility

Slow depth movement and at most one optional interactive scene. Ordinary scrolling, a visible Work button, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion support remain essential.

06 / Voice & messaging

Confident and specific. Use audience, culture, build, explore, strategy, craft. Avoid revolutionary, limitless, guru, guaranteed results, and unsupported superlatives.

I turn human insight into brands, campaigns, and worlds worth exploring.
