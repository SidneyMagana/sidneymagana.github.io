Creative case study / AI-assisted fashion ideation

From Forgotten
Fabric to New Form

What if an overlooked silk scarf could become something entirely new—without losing the character that made it special?

Creative directionGenerative AIMaterial reuseAdobe Express

01 / The brief

One scarf. Multiple possibilities.

I began with a beloved silk scarf and explored how its print, drape and visual identity might translate into wearable tops. The goal was not to manufacture a garment, but to develop a cohesive collection of speculative concepts through AI-assisted iteration.

Creative question

How can generative tools help visualize material reuse while preserving the recognizable qualities of the original fabric?

My role: concept development, creative direction, prompt refinement, silhouette selection and editorial presentation.

02 / Design exploration

The transformation

01. The source

Study the scarf's pattern, colors, texture and potential drape before changing its form.

02. The silhouettes

Explore spaghetti-strap crop top, handkerchief-hem tube top, bralette-inspired top, halter tie and asymmetrical scarf top concepts.

03. The edit

Compare visual coherence, wearability cues and the extent to which each concept retains the source material's identity.

These designs are AI-assisted visual concepts, not photographs of completed physical garments or verified sewing patterns.

03 / Process

Creative judgment stays human.

Assess — identify the scarf's defining qualities.
Explore — prompt and compare possible silhouettes and drapes.
Decide — refine toward wearable, minimal, versatile forms.
Present — build an editorial narrative in Adobe Express.

What the experiment demonstrates

Art direction, iteration, selection and storytelling—not simply generating attractive outputs.

The visual boards provided for this project include a six-look transformation sheet and a full editorial case-study composition. The final image gallery can be added once the source assets are hosted in the website repository.

04 / Outcome and reflection

A collection of possibilities.

The result is a visual series that reimagines one scarf across multiple forms, supported by a documented decision-making process and an editorial presentation. The project illustrates how AI can accelerate exploration while leaving taste, constraints and final selection with the creator.

“A piece with memories, reimagined to wear and love again.”
