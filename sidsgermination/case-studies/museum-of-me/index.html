Sidsgermination: A Museum of Me

My first immersive world, built without traditional 3D training

Independent creative exploration · Blender + New Art City

Explore the virtual world ↗

01 / Case study

The concept

What would a museum look like if its collection were personal memories, places, interests, and imagined environments? I built Sidsgermination as a museum of me—an explorable space shaped by things I love, have seen, and wanted to imagine.

02 / Case study

Turn curiosity into objects

I created pyramids, airplanes, vases, blocks, three-dimensional images, video, landscapes, and waterfalls—my favorite element. Each became a way to experiment with atmosphere, memory, and spatial composition.

03 / Case study

Learn by building

Without traditional 3D training, I used Blender and New Art City to build and assemble my environment. I learned tools through the act of making rather than waiting until I felt like an expert.

04 / Case study

A personal world in a connected exhibition

The Sidsgermination space and its artwork are entirely my own. It was displayed in a shared exhibition with portals leading to other independently authored worlds built by my professor and selected students. The connected exhibition did not change authorship of my individual space.

05 / Case study

Outcome and reflection

I completed an original, publicly accessible virtual environment. The project showed me that immersive storytelling can begin with curiosity and personal experience, then become tangible through iterative learning.

The Sidsgermination world and artwork are my own. Other linked exhibition worlds belong to their respective creators. New Art City is the hosting platform.
