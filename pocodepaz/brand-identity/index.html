Brand identity · 2026 · Proposed direction

Order, care,
and a little peace.

A property-care identity designed to feel composed, attentive and practical—distinct from Poco de Paz's earlier natural-products exploration.

01 / Brand foundation

Care for the details that keep a home running.

Audience

Owners of homes and small residential portfolios; tenants are an essential service audience.

Promise

Clear updates and organized care.

Voice

Composed, practical, attentive. Coordinate. Update. Follow through. Maintain.

02 / Color system

Quietly architectural.

Chalk #F5F1E8 · Midnight Slate #203747 · Harbor #325F74 · Olive #66704C · Terracotta #B65A46

Typography

Source Serif Pro for editorial headings; Acumin Pro for accessible body copy. System fallbacks are used until licensed web fonts are configured.

Mark

A restrained architectural roofline or arch, with the original Poco de Paz LLC mark retained as a historical identity asset. Supplied design studies are concept visuals, not photographs of managed properties.

03 / Design rationale

A little more peace in property care.

Warm chalk creates room to breathe. Slate and harbor establish trust and structure. Olive and terracotta provide restrained human warmth. Minimal motion, clear service lists and documented examples prioritize utility over spectacle.

04 / Evolution and archive

One name, distinct histories.

This identity is a proposed property-care direction, not an announced relaunch. Poco de Paz's natural-products history remains part of the brand's archive and should have a separate customer journey if revived.
