01 / Poco de Paz LLC · 2023–2026

Coordinating residential property operations

Role. Sidney oversaw day-to-day property operations and coordinated competing priorities with independent judgment and direct accountability.

02 / Volunteers of America · Lord Tennyson Apartments

Making affordable housing workflows more workable

Challenge. A recertification backlog and fragmented resident-notification and work-order processes at a community of approximately 400 units.

Role. Progressed from leasing to acting property management.

Approach. Organized appointments and resident notifications; paired paper-based maintenance workflows with software tracking.

Outcome. The recertification backlog was cleared and administrative workflows became more structured.
