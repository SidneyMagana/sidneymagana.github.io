Good questions. Clear answers.

What happens on a free fit call?

A 15-minute fit conversation covers your needs, budget, scope, and timing. It helps us choose a package; custom strategy and written recommendations belong in the paid session. Scheduling is arranged after your inquiry.

How do revisions work?

One round means one consolidated set of feedback from your designated approver, refining the agreed brief. A new audience, offer, format, or direction receives a separate quote before work begins.

What affects the timeline?

The clock begins after scope confirmation, complete materials, and payment/deposit. Feedback delays shift the schedule. We agree on the dates before production.

Do you use AI?

I use AI where it supports the work, with human direction, editing, and review. We agree on tool and confidentiality restrictions before production. Client claims and permissions still need approval.

Can we work in Spanish or Portuguese?

Language needs can be discussed during intake. Each package covers one agreed language; additional versions need separate scope and a review plan.

Do you manage publishing or guarantee sales?

The packages cover the listed strategy, production, and handoff. Publishing, advertising, and ongoing management are separate scope. Results depend on the offer, audience, distribution, and other factors.

Can you organize our creative workflow?

A Creative Workflow Setup service is being developed around intake, production tracking, review stages, and handoff. Ask about a scoped pilot; there is no posted fixed price yet.
