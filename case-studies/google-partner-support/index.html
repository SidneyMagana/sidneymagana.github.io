E-commerce operations · Applied AI

From Inbox to Self-Service.

Every support email showed us how a merchant described a problem and what it took to fix it. I turned that knowledge into internal articles and used it to train a chatbot for retail partners.

Google via Artech · Technical Project Manager · 2022–2023

01 / Problem

When products stopped publishing, merchants had to wait.

Retail partners emailed us about feed errors, platform integrations, quota increases, returns, and payment processing. Google wanted to give them a faster route to routine support through chat.

For complex issues, a useful answer started with getting the diagnosis right. A catalog could stop publishing because its data was wrong, its API connection had failed, or a product was restricted in the target market.

02 / Process & tools

I turned proven fixes into training.

I worked with two colleagues to study partner support emails, successful replies, and feedback from Qualtrics surveys. We looked for recurring questions, the words merchants used, and where they got stuck.

I wrote internal knowledge-base articles from those patterns and troubleshooting steps, then used that material in chatbot training. Our tools included partner support email, Qualtrics, and the internal knowledge base.

03 / Curated work sample

Getting large product feeds publishing again.

100,000–400,000 products per feed, approximately

When products stopped publishing, I traced the failure through the feed and its integration. I corrected or reworked feed data where I could and partnered with Engineering to resolve bugs and get affected feeds publishing again.

04 / Result

A self-service path and a support handoff.

Our three-person team completed the Partner Support training work. The resulting chat support gave merchants a self-service path for routine questions and supported smaller sales teams and third-party support centers. The training and knowledge base also helped transfer the support operation to a third-party team.
