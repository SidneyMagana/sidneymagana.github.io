Community engagement / Event production / North Richmond

Making room for

Behind a neighborhood Earth Day celebration: turning a familiar annual gathering into an accessible, coordinated day of environmental learning and connection.

North Richmond · 2019 · Retrospective case study

01 / Context

An established tradition, thoughtfully produced.

North Richmond's Earth Day celebration already existed. My contribution was to help produce a later edition: managing practical details, coordinating local partners and creating an engaging experience for residents of different ages.

That meant balancing an allocated budget, event permits, vendors, outreach, demonstrations, food and family-friendly entertainment.

My role

Event production and coordination, allocated-budget breakdown, permitting, vendor and partner logistics, complimentary offerings, community outreach and hands-on demonstration presenting.

Important distinction: I did not found or launch the festival.

03 / Production

From logistics to lived experience.

Plan

Break down the allocated budget, coordinate permissions and organize the vendor and activity schedule.

Invite

Work through existing community channels and participating organizations to help neighbors find the event.

Welcome

Coordinate barbecue, entertainment, face painting, balloons, family activities, free offerings and environmental demonstrations.

04 / My contribution

Not just behind the scenes.

I also served as a demonstration presenter, engaging directly with attendees. That combination—planning the infrastructure and participating in the experience—helped me understand how community events work from both sides.

What this demonstrates

Stakeholder coordination, budget stewardship, operational follow-through, public-facing communication and community-centered program delivery.

05 / Evidence & provenance

What the images represent.

A historical flyer supplied with this project advertises the 6th Annual North Richmond Green Festival & Earth Day Celebration on April 23, 2016. It establishes the earlier festival tradition, but does not document the 2019 edition or my specific role.

Editorial scenes showing families, gardening, face painting, balloons and barbecue are AI-generated conceptual reconstructions of the event atmosphere, not archival photographs.

These images should be displayed with captions preserving that distinction.

06 / Reflection

Good coordination creates space for connection.

For me, the meaningful result was a coordinated celebration where residents could learn, participate and spend time together. The project illustrates a broader approach I bring to operations work: practical structure in service of human experience.
